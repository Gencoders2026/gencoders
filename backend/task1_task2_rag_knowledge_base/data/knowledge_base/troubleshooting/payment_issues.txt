PAYMENT ISSUES - TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Payment Failed

Problem:
The customer's payment was declined or did not complete successfully.

Possible Causes:

Insufficient funds.
Incorrect card details.
Expired card.
Bank declined the transaction.
Payment gateway temporarily unavailable.
Billing address does not match the card information.
Transaction was flagged by the bank or payment provider.

Recommended Steps:

Verify the card number, expiration date, and CVV.
Confirm that sufficient funds are available.
Try the payment again after a few minutes.
Try another supported payment method.
Contact the bank or card issuer if the transaction continues to fail.

If the customer was charged but the order was not completed:

Do not make repeated payments immediately.
Check the account/order status.
Allow time for the payment provider to update the transaction.
Contact customer support with the transaction ID.
Payment Charged More Than Once

Problem:
The customer sees multiple charges for the same transaction.

Recommended Steps:

Check whether one of the charges is only a temporary authorization.
Verify the transaction IDs and timestamps.
Do not submit another payment.
Contact support with the payment details.

If duplicate charges are confirmed, the duplicate transaction should be investigated and refunded according to the Refund Policy.

Payment Pending

Problem:
The payment remains in a pending state.

Recommended Steps:

Wait for the payment provider to complete processing.
Do not repeatedly submit the same payment.
Check the order/account status.
If the payment remains pending for an extended period, contact support.
Refund Not Received

Problem:
The customer has been told that a refund was issued but has not received the funds.

Recommended Steps:

Confirm the refund date and amount.
Verify the original payment method.
Allow 5–10 business days for normal processing.
Contact the payment provider if the refund remains missing after the expected processing period.
Contact support with the original transaction ID if further investigation is required.
Billing Information Error

Problem:
The customer's billing information is incorrect.

Recommended Steps:

Update the billing information in the account.
Verify the billing address with the payment provider.
Retry the transaction if necessary.

When contacting support, provide:

Customer/account email.
Transaction or order ID.
Date and approximate time of payment.
Payment method used.
Description of the problem.

Never provide passwords, full card numbers, CVV codes, or other sensitive authentication information to support.
