DELIVERY AND ORDER TRACKING TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Delayed Order

Customers may contact support when an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period or when tracking information has not been updated.

Recommended Steps:

1. Confirm the customer's order number.
2. Check the latest order and shipment status.
3. Verify the estimated delivery date.
4. Check whether the tracking information has been updated recently.
5. If tracking has not updated for several days, advise the customer that the shipment may require additional carrier processing time.
6. Provide the customer with the latest available delivery estimate.
7. If the order is significantly delayed beyond the expected delivery date, contact the shipping provider or escalate the case according to support procedures.
8. Do not promise an exact delivery time unless the latest tracking information confirms it.

Recommended Customer Response:

Acknowledge the customer's concern about the delayed order.

Provide the latest available tracking or delivery information.

Give a clear next step and realistic timeline.

If the shipment remains delayed or tracking does not update, escalate the issue when appropriate.

Problem: Tracking Not Updated

If tracking information has not changed for several days:

1. Confirm the tracking number.
2. Check the latest carrier status.
3. Verify the expected delivery date.
4. Inform the customer that tracking updates may sometimes be delayed.
5. Provide the latest available information.
6. If there has been no update for an extended period, escalate the issue to the appropriate support or shipping team.

Important:

Never invent tracking information.

Never guarantee a delivery date without confirmed information.

Always provide the customer with a clear next step.
