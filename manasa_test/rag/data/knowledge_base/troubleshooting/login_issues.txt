LOGIN ISSUES - TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Cannot Log In

Problem:
The customer cannot access their account.

Recommended Steps:

Verify that the correct email address is being used.
Verify that the password is entered correctly.
Check whether Caps Lock is enabled.
Try signing in again.
Use the password reset option if the password may be incorrect.
Forgot Password

Problem:
The customer cannot remember their password.

Recommended Steps:

Select "Forgot Password" on the login page.
Enter the email address associated with the account.
Check the email inbox for the password reset message.
Check the spam or junk folder.
Follow the instructions in the reset email.

If the reset email does not arrive:

Confirm that the correct email address was entered.
Wait a few minutes and try again.
Contact customer support if the problem continues.
Account Locked

Problem:
The account has been temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.

Recommended Steps:

Wait for the temporary lockout period to expire.
Avoid repeatedly entering incorrect passwords.
Reset the password if necessary.
Contact support if the account remains inaccessible.
Login Verification Problem

Problem:
The customer cannot complete an email, OTP, or other verification step.

Recommended Steps:

Verify that the email address or phone number is correct.
Request a new verification code.
Use the most recently received code.
Check spam/junk folders for email verification messages.
Make sure the code has not expired.
Contact support if verification continues to fail.
Login Page Not Working

Problem:
The login page does not load correctly or produces an error.

Recommended Steps:

Refresh the page.
Clear browser cache and cookies.
Try a private/incognito browser window.
Try another supported browser.
Check the internet connection.
Disable browser extensions that may interfere with the login page.
Try again later if the service is temporarily unavailable.
Account Email Changed or Inaccessible

Problem:
The customer cannot access the email address associated with the account.

Recommended Steps:

Do not create a duplicate account unless instructed.
Contact customer support.
Provide sufficient account information to verify ownership.

Security Notice:

Support staff should never request or store a customer's password, authentication code, recovery code, or other sensitive credentials.

When contacting support, provide:

Account email address.
Description of the login problem.
Relevant error message.
Approximate time the issue occurred.
